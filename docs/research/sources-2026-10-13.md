## Clippy and mixed-initiative research

- https://www.erichorvitz.com/chi99horvitz.pdf: Principles of Mixed-Initiative User Interfaces (CHI 1999). Timing, deferral, invoke and terminate, "doing less", the LookOut agent that nods and disappears (read; local copy in the session's tool-results folder).
